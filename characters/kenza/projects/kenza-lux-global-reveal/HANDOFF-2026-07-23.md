## Kenza canon

- Adult virtual influencer, canonically 27.
- Venezuelan; global Miami positioning.
- Straight chin-length black bob; hazel/olive-green eyes; warm olive skin;
  defined red lips; black leather biker wardrobe.
- Facial rule: no pronounced eye bags, dark circles, puffiness, or deep tear
  troughs. Keep realistic skin texture.
- Identity rule: use the face only in close or medium-close frames. Wide frames
  use helmet, profile, silhouette, rear view, or face occlusion.

The generated medium-wide face is not a reliable identity anchor. The close face
reference is the production anchor. If an LTX render drifts, crop closer or use
a helmeted version.

## Motorcycle canon

Internal asset name: `KENZA-BUSA-01`.

- Current third-generation Hayabusa-inspired silhouette.
- Glass-sparkle black dominant body with matte-black secondary panels.
- Restrained metallic fuchsia `#F22987` accents and thin rim pinstripes.
- Custom fictional Kenza livery, not an official Suzuki colorway or announced
  collaboration.
- No sponsor graphics in this first Reel.

### Product lock

Do not ask LTX to redraw the motorcycle. LTX may animate light sweeps, smoke,
reflections, wet pavement, background movement, and very small adjacent parallax.
It must not regenerate fairing geometry, wheels, mechanical parts, badges, logos,
or readable lettering.

No generative wheel rotation, aggressive orbit, or large viewpoint change from a
single still. A moving hero motorcycle requires real footage or an exact 3D asset.

### Brand lock

Approved logos must be applied after AI generation from SVG or transparent PNG
masters using planar tracking, corner pinning, masks, matched motion blur, and
lighting/color integration.
