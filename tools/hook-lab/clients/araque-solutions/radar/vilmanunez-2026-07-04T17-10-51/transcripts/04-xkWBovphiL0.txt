Reach your goal your way
https://www.youtube.com/shorts/xkWBovphiL0
========================================
This message is for you.  It doesn't matter
how fast you go, the important thing is to
reach the finish line.  When I started, I
compared myself, and you have to understand something:
competition is evidence that you
can do it too, but you don't have to do it with
the same sacrifice, pace, and
speed as others.  You have to
do it however you feel comfortable,
because at the end of the day the important thing is to
reach the goal and stay at the goal,
not to go up and down.
