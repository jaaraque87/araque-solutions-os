## Mechanical correction: clutch

The third-generation Hayabusa uses a **hydraulic clutch**. A compact clutch
master cylinder and fluid reservoir on the left handlebar can be legitimate.
The problem is not the reservoir's existence; the problem is when an image model
places it incorrectly, floats it in front of the handlebar, duplicates it, or
breaks its connection to the lever.

Several AI clutch tests were rejected and were **not saved**:

- some showed the right-side front brake instead of the left clutch;
- some duplicated the clutch lever;
- some moved only half of the lever;
- some produced unnatural hand/finger motion.

Do not ask LTX to invent a mechanically exact clutch pull from one still.

Preferred solutions for Shot 3:

1. real iPhone footage of a third-generation Hayabusa left hydraulic clutch;
2. an exact 3D motorcycle/control asset;
3. two truly matched photographic keyframes with the complete rigid lever
   rotating around the same hinge pivot.

For real footage, crop tightly enough that custom body branding is not required.

Shot 4 should also use real footage or an exact asset if it contains a physical
boot/control interaction. Do not generate a mechanically ambiguous shifter,
brake pedal, or wheel assembly.
