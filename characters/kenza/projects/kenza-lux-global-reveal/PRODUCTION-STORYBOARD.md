# Kenza Lux Global Reveal — Production Storyboard

## Production rules

- Duration: 20 seconds, vertical 1080x1920, English-first.
- Kenza's canonical face appears only in close or medium-close shots.
- Wide shots use helmet, profile, silhouette, rear view, or face occlusion.
- KENZA-BUSA-01 is a locked black Gen III Hayabusa product plate with metallic
  fuchsia accents. LTX may animate atmosphere and adjacent parallax, not redraw
  motorcycle geometry, badges, or sponsor marks.
- Sponsor logos are excluded from this first Reel. Future approved logos are
  composited after generation from vector or transparent PNG masters.
- B-roll is generated without dialogue audio. Hero character clips may use audio
  only when a spoken version is approved.

## Shot sequence

### S01 — Ignition / Hook

- Time: 00:00.00–00:02.20
- Role: Pattern interrupt.
- Visual: Smartphone macro of the black front fairing, windscreen edge, fuchsia
  accent line, and headlight reflection. No face.
- Camera: Slow 4% push-in; locked product plate.
- LTX: Animate only light sweep, faint smoke, and wet-asphalt reflections.
- On-screen copy: `THE FUTURE`
- Audio: Ignition click, low engine pulse, first music impact.
- End frame: Fuchsia highlight wipes right.

### S02 — Canon Face Reveal

- Time: 00:02.20–00:05.20
- Role: Establish Kenza.
- Visual: Medium-close UGC portrait, chest-up, Kenza beside the tank and
  handlebar. Her face occupies enough pixels to preserve identity.
- Camera: iPhone 16 Pro main-camera look, subtle handheld drift under 1.5%.
- Identity: Rested under-eye contour; no bags, dark circles, puffiness, or deep
  tear troughs. Exact black bob, hazel/olive eyes, red lips, warm olive skin.
- LTX: Minimal breathing, natural blink, micro head movement only. No face turn.
- On-screen copy: `KENZA LUX`
- Secondary copy: `MIAMI / VIRTUAL / BIKER`
- Audio: Engine settles under the beat.

### S03A — Glove / Control

- Time: 00:05.20–00:06.40
- Role: Tactile biker proof.
- Visual: Gloved hand naturally resting on the left grip beside the clutch lever.
- Camera: Tight smartphone insert.
- LTX: Micro finger pressure and reflection movement only; no control deformation.
- Copy: `LUXURY.`

### S03B — Boot / Machine

- Time: 00:06.40–00:07.60
- Role: Physical confidence.
- Visual: Black boot grounded beside the bike; fuchsia rim pinstripe enters frame.
- Camera: Low macro crop with locked wheel geometry.
- LTX: Animate puddle reflection and light, not wheel rotation.
- Copy: `SPEED.`

### S03C — Helmet / Visor

- Time: 00:07.60–00:09.20
- Role: Complete the iconography.
- Visual: Black helmet and visor held near the tank; Kenza's face is out of frame.
- Camera: Short lateral slide created as 2.5D parallax.
- LTX: Reflection travel across visor; no helmet reshaping.
- Copy: `ATTITUDE.`

### S04 — Kenza + Busa Signature

- Time: 00:09.20–00:12.80
- Role: Hero brand image.
- Visual: Medium-wide UGC frame showing Kenza and approximately three quarters of
  KENZA-BUSA-01: front wheel, fairing, windscreen, tank, handlebar, and rear cowl.
- Camera: Closer than a full-bike shot; no extreme low angle.
- Identity: Use canon face anchor. If the face drifts, replace this with helmeted
  profile or crop closer rather than accepting an inconsistent face.
- Ergonomics: One hand supported by the handlebar, relaxed shoulder and elbow;
  one boot grounded; hips aligned with seat and tank.
- LTX: Atmosphere and camera-adjacent parallax only. Motorcycle remains locked.
- Copy: None; allow the image to breathe.

### S05 — Origin to Global

- Time: 00:12.80–00:16.30
- Role: Position Kenza internationally.
- Visual: Helmeted three-quarter silhouette with Miami lights and wet asphalt.
  Face is not identity-critical.
- Camera: Slow over-shoulder drift, no orbit around the motorcycle.
- LTX: Smoke, hair tips, jacket edge, background light movement.
- Copy relay:
  - `VENEZUELAN FIRE.`
  - `MIAMI NIGHTS.`
  - `GLOBAL STAGE.`
- Audio: Three percussion accents.

### S06 — Brand Lockup / CTA

- Time: 00:16.30–00:20.00
- Role: Recognition and follow action.
- Visual: Locked hero crop dissolves into black with one fuchsia light trace.
- Camera: Static hold for readability.
- LTX: None on final lockup.
- Copy:
  - `KENZA LUX`
  - `MIAMI'S VIRTUAL BIKER ICON`
  - `FOLLOW THE RIDE`
  - `@KENZALUX.OFFICIAL`
- Audio: Final engine tail and music resolve.
