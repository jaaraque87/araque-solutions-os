# Short-Form Video: Hooks, Scripts & Strategy
Referencia para TikTok, Reels y YouTube Shorts.

## Video Hook Library

### Curiosity Hooks (mejor para engagement)
- **Secret:** "The secret to [outcome] that nobody talks about" · "I found the hidden feature in [X] that changes everything" · "I can't believe this actually works…"
- **Unexpected Discovery:** "I tried [X] for 30 days and I was NOT expecting this" · "This completely changed how I think about [topic]" · "Nobody talks about this, but…"
- **Question:** "Why does nobody talk about this?" · "Am I the only one who didn't know this?" · "The reason [common thing] doesn't work is…"

### Value Hooks (mejor para saves)
- **Promise:** "How to [outcome] in [timeframe]" · "[N] [things] that will [benefit]" · "Everything you need to know about [topic] in 60 seconds"
- **Hack/Shortcut:** "[Outcome] hack that actually works" · "The [adj] way to [outcome]" · "If you're struggling with [problem], watch this"
- **Warning:** "Stop doing [practice] — here's why" · "[N] mistakes that are killing your [results]" · "Why [thing you think is good] is actually hurting you"

### Story Hooks (mejor para watch time)
- **Transformation:** "3 months ago I [bad state]. Today I [good state]." · "Here's how I went from [before] to [after]" · "I used to think [old belief]. Then [event] changed everything."
- **Failure:** "I made a huge mistake with [topic]" · "Here's why I stopped [practice]" · "I lost [something] because of this mistake"
- **Journey:** "So this just happened…" · "This changed everything for me" · "Let me tell you about the time I [situation]"

### Controversial Hooks (mejor para comentarios)
- "Unpopular opinion: [bold statement]" · "[Common advice] is actually wrong" · "I'm going to get hate for this, but…" · "Most [audience] get this completely wrong"

## Scripting Template
```
## Video: [Título]
Platform: TikTok / Reels / Shorts · Length: XX seg · Format: [Talking head / Slideshow / Demo / Screen rec]

### Hook (0-3 sec)
- Visual: [qué ve el espectador]
- Audio: [qué escucha]
- Text overlay: [texto en pantalla]

### Body (3-X sec)
- [Timestamp] - [qué pasa / qué decís]
- [Timestamp] - [siguiente beat]

### CTA (últimos 3-5 sec)
- Verbal: [qué decís] · Text: [en pantalla] · Action: [follow, comment, link in bio]

### Production Notes
- Music/sound · B-roll · Graphics
```

## Estructuras adicionales
- **Story Arc (45-60s):** [0-3] hook teasea el resultado · [3-15] setup/stakes · [15-45] journey · [45-55] resolución · [55-60] lección/CTA. (Historias personales, case studies, testimonios.)
- **POV/Skit (15-30s):** [0-3] setup con text overlay · [3-25] actuar el escenario relatable · [25-30] punchline/twist.

## Patrones visuales
- **Talking head:** buena luz (ring/ventana), contacto visual, gestos, fondo interesante.
- **Slideshow:** visual fuerte por slide (2-4s), text overlays, estilo consistente, voiceover o trending sound.
- **Screen recording:** zoom a lo importante, resaltar cursor, movimientos suaves, cara en esquina (opcional).
- **B-roll heavy:** show don't tell, cortes rápidos (1-3s), match cuts a los beats de la voz, mezclar wide/medium/close-up.

## Audio
- **Trending sounds:** entretenimiento/lifestyle, cuando el trend sube. Evitar si distrae o ya declina.
- **Original audio:** educativo hablando, storytimes, demos/tutoriales, construir voz de marca reconocible. *(← este es el caso del método Kumar)*
- **Voiceover:** hablar algo más rápido, variar tono, pausar para énfasis, grabar en silencio + noise removal, voces IA para faceless (ElevenLabs).
- **Música:** matchear energía, evitar copyright en Reels/Shorts, usar librerías de plataforma, bajar volumen bajo la voz (ducking).

## Posting
- **Horarios:** TikTok 7-9AM/12-3PM/7-11PM · Reels 9AM/12PM/7-9PM · Shorts 12-3PM/7-9PM.
- **Frecuencia:** creciendo 2-4/día · manteniendo 1/día · testeando 5/sem.
- **Batch:** ideate (30min) → script (1h) → batch film (2h) → edit+captions (2-3h) → schedule (30min).

## Métricas y test
Watch time %, completion rate, saves, shares, comments, follows. Testear: hooks, longitud, formato, horario, CTA.
**Cuándo pivotear:** 5+ videos con <1% completion → cambiar hooks · muchas views pocos follows → revisar CTA y fit · muchos saves pocos shares → valioso pero no social · muchos comentarios negativos → ajustar tono.
