# Seven-shot phone-native design

| Shot | Frame and phone owner | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walking arm-length selfie | Person, place, tension, hook |
| 2 | Downward handheld POV | Product/tray enters by a visible hand |
| 3 | Handheld macro | First verifiable product action |
| 4 | Handheld sensory macro | Most satisfying physical change |
| 5 | Handheld overhead macro | Differentiating step or result |
| 6 | Phone propped on table | Full-face use or reaction |
| 7 | Same propped framing | Product turn and natural CTA |

Preserve framing and face scale in shots 6–7. Adapt shots 3–5 to the real procedure.

For each shot record duration, frame, phone owner, handheld/propped state, initial state, action, final state, speaker, exact line, on-camera status, silent mouth state, object counts, product physics, prohibitions, and audio events.

Keep the total near 30 seconds, dialogue near 68 words, and on-camera lines at 13 words or fewer. Use the client's dialect.

Before approval, verify order, parts, utensils, temperature, timing, quantities, object states, forbidden failures, and parts needing their own references. State correct behavior plus explicit negative constraints.
