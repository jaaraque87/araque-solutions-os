# Post Format Templates

## LinkedIn
**Story Post:** Hook (resultado/lección inesperada) → escena (cuándo/dónde) → desafío → qué probaste/qué pasó → punto de giro → resultado → lección → pregunta.
**Contrarian Take:** opinión impopular audaz → "Here's why:" 3 razones → qué recomendás en cambio → "Am I wrong?".
**List Post:** "[X] things I learned about [topic] after [credibilidad]:" → 1/2/3 punto + explicación breve → insight de cierre → "Which resonates most?".
**How-To:** "How to [outcome] in [timeframe]:" → Step 1/2/3 (Action + ↳ por qué / detalle / error a evitar) → resultado esperado → CTA.

## Twitter/X
**Tutorial Thread:** T1 hook + promesa ("Here's exactly how to [outcome] step-by-step:") → T2-7 un paso por tweet → final resumen + "follow me for more on [topic]".
**Story Thread:** T1 hook ("[Time] ago, [algo inesperado]. Here's the full story:") → T2-6 beats con tensión → T7 resolución + lección → final takeaway + ask.
**Breakdown Thread:** T1 "[Company/person] just [did thing]. Here's why it's genius:" → T2-6 análisis → T7 takeaway + follow CTA.

## Instagram
**Carousel Hook:** Slide 1 afirmación/pregunta audaz → slides 2-9 un punto por slide (visual + texto) → slide 10 resumen + CTA → caption expande + CTA.
**Reel Script:** Hook (0-2s) pattern interrupt/claim audaz → Setup (2-5s) contexto → Value (5-25s) el consejo → CTA (25-30s) follow/comment/share/link.

## Hook Formulas
- **Curiosity:** "I was wrong about [creencia]." · "The real reason [outcome] happens isn't what you think." · "[Resultado] — and it only took [tiempo corto]." · "Nobody talks about [insider knowledge]."
- **Story:** "Last week, [algo inesperado] happened." · "I almost [error]." · "3 years ago I [pasado]. Today [presente]." · "[Person] told me something I'll never forget."
- **Value:** "How to [outcome] (without [dolor]):" · "[N] [things] that [outcome]:" · "The simplest way to [outcome]:" · "Stop [error]. Do this instead:"
- **Contrarian:** "Unpopular opinion: [X]" · "[Consejo común] is wrong. Here's why:" · "I stopped [práctica] and [resultado]." · "Everyone says [X]. The truth is [Y]."
- **Social Proof:** "We [resultado] in [tiempo]. Here's the full story:" · "[N] people asked me about [topic]. Here's my answer:" · "[Autoridad] taught me [lección]."
