# KENZA LUX — GLOBAL REVEAL

## Style block

- Design system: `frame.md` / **Kenza After Dark**
- Canvas: 1080 × 1920 vertical
- Palette: background `#080808`, ink `#F2EEE8`, muted `#A7A29B`,
  surface `#222326`, metal `#55565B`, accent `#C6202E`
- Display: League Gothic 400, uppercase, tightly tracked
- Supporting copy: Space Mono 400/700
- Mood: fashion-film restraint, superbike mechanical precision, Miami after dark
- Concept angle: the future of biker culture enters as a woman-shaped brand icon

## Rhythm declaration

`ignite — REVEAL — accelerate — declare — HOLD`

The first three seconds create tension, the identity lands by second four, the
middle accelerates through sensory evidence, and the final lockup receives the
longest clean hold.

## Global rules

- Keep one dominant photographic focal point and one typographic focal point.
- Preserve 72px horizontal and 150px vertical safe areas.
- Use close crops and silhouettes until Kenza's face is intentionally revealed.
- Kenza remains visually canonical: black bob, hazel/olive eyes, red lips,
  athletic adult build, black leather jacket, black gloves, boots, and black helmet.
- Every scene carries 2–5 atmosphere layers: grain, headlight bloom, brushed-metal
  hairlines, crop marks, smoke, wet-asphalt reflections, or oversized ghost type.
- Racing red appears once per scene as warning light, taillight, lipstick, or rule.
- Camera motion stays controlled; type and mechanical details provide impact.
- Use velocity-matched lateral handoffs and hard editorial cuts.
- No narration. Music and motorcycle sound design drive timing.

## Scene 1 — Ignition / Hook — 3.2s

### Concept

The viewer enters a black frame before the machine is visible. A narrow red
headlight slit and a low engine pulse awaken the darkness while the statement
"THE FUTURE / DOESN'T ARRIVE QUIETLY." assembles in two brutal beats.

### Mood direction

Luxury automotive teaser meets a noir title sequence. The pause before ignition
is as important as the impact.

### Depth layers

- BG: tinted black, low vignette, slow smoke drift, coarse film grain.
- MG: red headlight slit and a barely legible helmet silhouette.
- FG: large League Gothic hook, crop marks, tiny `MIAMI / 25.7617° N` metadata.

### Animation choreography

- Headlight BLOOMS from a hairline to a narrow red beam.
- "THE FUTURE" SLAMS upward from blur and locks sharply.
- "DOESN'T ARRIVE QUIETLY." SNAPS in as a second beat.
- Crop marks CLICK into position; smoke DRIFTS continuously.

### Transition out

Velocity-matched whip to the right, 0.35s, with directional blur peaking at cut.

## Scene 2 — Identity / Reveal — 4.0s

### Concept

The whip resolves into Kenza's three-quarter portrait holding the helmet. The
camera settles from an aggressive close crop while the name KENZA LUX occupies
the lower third like a magazine masthead.

### Mood direction

International fashion campaign, not social-media selfie. Controlled confidence.

### Depth layers

- BG: black-to-gunmetal garage void with one soft headlight bloom.
- MG: canonical Kenza portrait, edge-lit in red and cold neutral light.
- FG: `KENZA LUX`, a thin red rule, and `MIAMI / VIRTUAL / BIKER`.

### Animation choreography

- Portrait CLEARS from rack-focus while the camera PUSHES in 4%.
- `KENZA` WIPES upward from a hard mask; `LUX` LOCKS beside it in red.
- Metadata TYPES ON mechanically.
- The helmet highlight BREATHES once and settles.

### Transition out

Hard editorial cut on a metallic impact; the red rule becomes the next scene's
taillight streak.

## Scene 3 — Machine / Evidence — 4.0s

### Concept

Three macro fragments — helmet visor, gloved hand on metal, boot beside the
machine — flash as tactile proof of the world. The fragments pivot aside for the
statement `LUXURY. SPEED. ATTITUDE.`

### Mood direction

High-end motorcycle commercial cut like a fashion editorial contact sheet.

### Depth layers

- BG: wet asphalt reflections, garage haze, oversized ghost word `MACHINE`.
- MG: three full-bleed macro crops in rapid sequence.
- FG: metal dividers, index labels `01 / 02 / 03`, hero statement.

### Animation choreography

- Macro crops CUT on three percussion hits with slight push-ins.
- Dividers DRAW across the frame like measurement marks.
- Media group SLIDES left and yields the center.
- `LUXURY. SPEED. ATTITUDE.` arrives in a WATERFALL and holds.

### Transition out

Blur-through crossfade, 0.30s, preserving the leftward velocity.

## Scene 4 — Origin to Global — 4.0s

### Concept

Kenza becomes more than a look. A fixed silhouette anchors the right edge while
three identity statements replace one another on the left:
`VENEZUELAN FIRE.` → `MIAMI NIGHTS.` → `GLOBAL STAGE.`

### Mood direction

Luxury manifesto with the cadence of a racetrack starting sequence.

### Depth layers

- BG: dark Miami geometry and a subtle horizon glow.
- MG: Kenza full-body silhouette with helmet, boots, and jacket.
- FG: identity relay, red registration dot, longitude/latitude chrome.

### Animation choreography

- Silhouette HOLDS with a slow 2% push-in.
- Each statement HARD-CUTS into the same anchor with a distinct motion:
  SLAM, SIDE-SNAP, then RISE-AND-LOCK.
- Red dot PULSES once per statement.
- Background hairlines DRIFT laterally at different depths.

### Transition out

The final red dot EXPANDS into a near-black color dip, 0.35s, revealing the lockup.

## Scene 5 — Brand Lockup / CTA — 4.8s

### Concept

On a cleared stage, the name assembles as if machined from metal. `KENZA` and
`LUX` lock together, the red underline draws on, and the descriptor plus CTA
arrive beneath. The final frame is held long enough to remember.

### Mood direction

Premium automotive end card with fashion-house discipline.

### Depth layers

- BG: clean tinted black, grain, a restrained radial bloom.
- MG: monumental wordmark with brushed-metal ghost extrusion.
- FG: red underline, `MIAMI'S VIRTUAL BIKER ICON`, `FOLLOW THE RIDE`, `@KENZALUX.OFFICIAL`.

### Animation choreography

- Letter groups ASSEMBLE from opposing sides and interlock.
- Red underline DRAWS left to right.
- Descriptor WIPES in; CTA and handle RISE together.
- All elements settle into a dead-static 2.2s recognition hold.

### Transition out

No exit animation. End on the held lockup.

## Recurring motifs

- Racing-red line transforms across scenes: headlight → masthead rule → taillight
  streak → registration pulse → final underline.
- Small coordinate and machine-index labels establish a global editorial system.
- Kenza's red lips echo the sole accent color without becoming a beauty ad.
- Mechanical lock-ins and camera-focus clears repeat as the motion grammar.

## Audio identity

- 0.0s: sub-bass ignition swell.
- 1.0s: engine catch plus metallic type hit.
- 3.2s: whip pass.
- 7.2–10.2s: three percussive mechanical clicks for macro cuts.
- 11.2–15.2s: restrained pulse under the identity relay.
- 15.2s: low impact and short metallic resonance.
- 17.8–20.0s: music simplifies so the lockup owns the close.

## Negative prompt

- No generic purple/cyan cyberpunk, beauty-influencer gradients, rounded UI cards,
  excessive glitch, fake sponsor logos, unsafe riding, brand-performance claims,
  juvenile styling, explicit sexuality, or inconsistent face/body proportions.
- Do not conceal that Kenza is a virtual character.
