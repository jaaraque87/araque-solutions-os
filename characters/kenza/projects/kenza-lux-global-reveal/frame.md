## Overview

Kenza After Dark is a global luxury-biker title system: fashion-film restraint
collides with the mechanical precision of a superbike garage. The frame feels
expensive, nocturnal, and dangerous without becoming chaotic or cyberpunk.

## The Frame

Use deep, tinted black as a constant canvas. Kenza or the motorcycle is the
dominant photographic focal point; monumental condensed typography becomes the
second focal point. Crop boldly and anchor subjects to an edge so every vertical
frame feels like a magazine cover caught in motion.

## Composition Rules

- Keep all essential copy inside the 72px horizontal and 150px vertical safe area.
- Use no more than six words in a hero statement.
- Let the subject occupy 55–75% of the frame at the key poster moment.
- Use one racing-red focal hit per scene; neutrals carry everything else.
- Hairline rules, crop marks, and small coordinate labels provide mechanical detail.
- Portraits must preserve Kenza's canonical black bob, hazel/olive eyes, red lips,
  athletic hourglass build, black leather jacket, black boots, gloves, and helmet.

## Motion Character

Entrances feel like a camera finding its subject: focus clears, headlights sweep,
type locks into the grid, and mechanical details snap into place. Holds breathe
slowly. Transitions accelerate through the cut and settle with matched velocity.

## Do

- Use close crops, silhouette, reflections, metal, leather, smoke, and wet asphalt.
- Combine controlled camera push-ins with percussive editorial cuts.
- Preserve readable type at mobile-feed scale.
- Make the red accent feel like a taillight, warning light, or lipstick signature.

## Do Not

- No cyan/purple generic neon.
- No soft beauty-influencer gradients or rounded UI cards.
- No fake sponsor logos, motorcycle badges, or product-performance claims.
- No explicit sexuality, juvenile styling, photorealistic impersonation, or
  ambiguity about Kenza being an adult virtual character.
